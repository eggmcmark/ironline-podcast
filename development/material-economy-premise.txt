THE FUTURE ECONOMY — FRESH START
Recorded 2026-10-01 from Mark's direction. Development notes, not a finished story bible.

CORE PREMISE
A vast blockchain-linked archive tags or tracks physical objects and matter, natural or manufactured. Scarce objects can be traded for corresponding blockchain value. This includes ordinary nuts and bolts, stone, wood, building materials, and specialized streams such as wood offcuts collected for reprocessing.

Expertise in recognizing, recovering, combining, and transforming parts and waste into desirable products enriches the economy. Matter accumulates complex, beautiful provenance as objects change, split, combine, and acquire new uses. This process belongs at the center of the story.

WORLD AND TONE
Mark wants an accessible, connected world, potentially with many fewer people. Abandoned cities can contain small communities among aging architecture, with ordinary freedom to visit and travel. Do not infer secret borders, inaccessible territories, civilizational fracture, or apocalyptic misery from a smaller population. Curious, inventive, inhabited, materially specific; room for humor, pleasure, and real human disagreements.

RETIRED
The entire previous narrative is archived. Do not carry forward the Compact/Outside split, conspiracy, grandmother/key quest, Curator, collapse chronology, or delayed-revelation structure. No character or old world rule is approved for reuse. No predetermined four-season or forty-minute format.

POSSIBLE KEEPERS — PROPOSALS ONLY
The working title; sensory precision; respect for skilled makers; understated humor; a materials-literate viewpoint; spacious cities; ordinary AI-supported logistics. These are qualities, not inherited canon.

DESIGN QUESTIONS — STILL OPEN
How are identity, custody, condition, and physical verification linked to records? How do splits, combinations, losses, and transformations update provenance without counting the same matter twice? How are prices discovered, and what exactly does an on-chain claim entitle its holder to? A recorded history need not make every object valuable. Scarcity, usefulness, demand, verification, transport, and processing costs require consideration.

NEXT CREATIVE STEP
Choose people who want to make or accomplish something concrete within this economy. Let their decisions and material transformations drive events, with satisfying results along the way. No replacement plot has been selected or authorized as canon.
